BPAAU Review Methodology

By Daniel Hart · Senior Pokies Reviewer & iGaming Editor · Last updated 29 August 2026

Editorial methodology · Testing process · Scoring criteria · Last updated 29 August 2026

This document explains exactly how BPAAU tests, scores, and ranks online pokies operators. We publish our methodology so that players, regulators, and operators can understand the basis for every recommendation on this site. Our process is independent, repeatable, and evidence-based.

Core editorial principles

Independence

We do not accept payment for rankings, review placement, or removal of negative findings. Affiliate commissions never influence editorial decisions.

Evidence-based

Every claim is backed by a test we performed or a source we cite. We do not reproduce operator marketing as fact.

Transparency

We disclose affiliate relationships, show our scoring criteria, and clearly mark operator-provided vs. independently tested information.

Accountability

Confirmed errors are corrected within 48 hours. Material corrections are noted at the top of the affected page with date and nature of change.

The 6-step testing process

Every operator listed on BPAAU completes all six steps before publication. Steps are performed by different team members where possible to reduce bias.

  1. Licence verification Performed by Daniel Hart. We confirm the operator holds an active Gaming Curacao licence by checking the regulator's public register. We record the licence number, issue date, and current status. We also check for any regulatory sanctions or licence suspensions in the past 24 months. Operators without a verifiable, active licence are immediately rejected — they never reach step 2.
  2. Registration & KYC test Performed by Sarah Chen. A team member completes full registration using a real Australian identity, including passport/driver's licence upload and proof of address. We measure KYC approval time in hours, flag any operator that requests excessive documentation (beyond standard ID + address), and record whether verification can be completed entirely online or requires manual review. KYC delays beyond 48 hours result in a score deduction.
  3. Payment channel test Performed by Sarah Chen. We make a real deposit (minimum $20 AUD) and a real withdrawal using each advertised payment method. For PayID — the most common Australian method — we perform three separate withdrawal cycles and record the median processing time. We also record: deposit/withdrawal fees, minimum and maximum limits, whether the method is available at both deposit and withdrawal, and any currency conversion charges. Payment channels that are advertised but unavailable at withdrawal are flagged as misleading.
  4. Bonus term audit Performed by Daniel Hart. We cross-check every advertised bonus against the operator's published terms and conditions. For each bonus we record: wagering multiplier (on bonus amount, winnings, or deposit+bonus), maximum cashout cap, expiry window (from activation and from wagering start), game contribution weightings (slots vs. table games vs. live dealer), maximum bet during wagering, and any excluded game categories. We then calculate the "effective value" of each bonus — the expected realisable value after accounting for wagering probability and max cashout. Bonuses with terms that are objectively unfair (e.g., 100x+ wagering, $0 max cashout, 24-hour expiry on high-wagering bonuses) are flagged with a warning.
  5. Game library assessment Performed by Marcus Webb. We audit the game library across five dimensions: (1) provider coverage — number of distinct game providers and presence of major providers (Pragmatic Play, Play'n GO, NetEnt, etc.); (2) pokie count — total number of slot games and number of games with Australian-facing titles; (3) RTP distribution — the range and median RTP of available pokies, with a check for whether operators reduce RTP on specific games; (4) mechanic coverage — availability of Megaways, Hold & Win, progressive jackpots, and buy-in bonus features; (5) mobile performance — we test at least 10 games on both iOS Safari and Android Chrome, recording load time, frame stability, and touch responsiveness. Games that fail to load within 10 seconds on a 4G connection are noted.
  6. Support responsiveness test Performed by Emma Rodriguez. We contact customer support via live chat and email with a standard set of five questions covering: bonus terms, PayID withdrawal process, account verification, responsible gambling tools, and complaint resolution. We measure: first response time (live chat), full resolution time (email), accuracy of answers (compared against published terms), and whether the support agent proactively offers responsible gambling resources. Operators that fail to respond to email within 24 hours, or whose live chat wait exceeds 10 minutes, receive a score deduction.

Scoring system

Each operator receives a total score out of 100, calculated from five weighted categories. The weights reflect what matters most to Australian players: trust and payout speed together account for 50% of the score.

CategoryWeightSub-criteriaData source
Licence & Trust25%Licence validity, regulator reputation, KYC fairness, complaint history, responsible gambling toolsSteps 1, 2, 6
Payout Speed & Methods25%PayID withdrawal time (median of 3 tests), deposit options, withdrawal options, fees, limitsStep 3
Bonus Fairness20%Wagering multiplier, max cashout, expiry, game weighting, max bet, effective bonus valueStep 4
Game Library20%Provider count, pokie count, RTP distribution, mechanic coverage, mobile performanceStep 5
Support & UX10%Response time, answer accuracy, support channels, site usability, mobile experienceStep 6

Score bands

  • 90–100 (Excellent): Outstanding across all categories. Recommended without reservation.
  • 75–89 (Good): Strong performance with minor weaknesses. Recommended with notes.
  • 60–74 (Fair): Acceptable but with notable issues in one or more categories. Listed with warnings.
  • Below 60 (Poor): Significant problems. Not recommended; may be delisted.

Ongoing monitoring and re-checks

An operator's initial score is not permanent. We maintain a continuous monitoring process:

Quarterly re-check (every 90 days): All six steps are repeated for every listed operator. Scores are recalculated and rankings updated. Changes of more than 10 points are noted on the operator's review page.
Monthly payment spot-check: Sarah Chen performs one PayID deposit/withdrawal cycle at a randomly selected operator each month to verify that payout speeds remain consistent with the tested baseline.
Real-time alert monitoring: We monitor player complaint forums, social media, and regulator announcements for signals that an operator's service has degraded. Trigger events include: licence suspension, payment channel closure, data breach, or a spike in withdrawal complaints.
Immediate review (within 7 days): If a trigger event is detected, we launch an immediate review. If the issue is confirmed, the operator is flagged with a warning, demoted in rankings, or delisted depending on severity.

What gets an operator delisted

  • Licence lapse or suspension: Immediate delisting. We do not list unlicensed operators under any circumstances.
  • Withdrawal non-payment: If we or a verified player experiences a withdrawal that is not processed within 14 days without a legitimate reason, the operator is delisted pending investigation.
  • False advertising: If an operator advertises a bonus, payment method, or game that does not exist or has materially different terms, and fails to correct it within 30 days of our notification.
  • Data breach or security failure: Confirmed breach of player data without adequate notification and remediation.
  • Spike in unresolved complaints: A sustained increase in player complaints that the operator fails to address.

Editorial independence policy

BPAAU's editorial independence is governed by the following rules, which are binding on all team members:

  • No team member may accept gifts, free credits, or preferential treatment from listed operators beyond what is available to any player.
  • Affiliate commission rates are not shared with the editorial team and are not a factor in scoring or ranking.
  • Operators may not review, approve, or edit content before publication. They may submit corrections after publication, which are evaluated against our corrections policy.
  • No operator may purchase a "review" or "feature" that is not clearly labelled as sponsored content. All sponsored content is marked with rel="sponsored" and a visible disclosure.
  • Team members must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a listed operator. Such relationships result in recusal from reviewing that operator.
