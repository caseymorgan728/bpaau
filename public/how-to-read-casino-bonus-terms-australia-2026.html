Wagering Requirements Explained (20x–40x)

Wagering requirements (also called playthrough or turnover) specify how many times you must bet the bonus amount before winnings become withdrawable. For example, a $20 free chips no deposit australia 2026 with 30x wagering requires $600 in total bets ($20 × 30). For free spins, wagering usually applies to the winnings from the spins, not the spin value. Typical ranges: 20x–40x for no deposit bonuses, 25x–35x for deposit matches. Lower is always better.

Important details: (1) What counts — only bets on eligible games count toward wagering. (2) Contribution rates — slots usually 100%, table games 10%–20%, live dealer often 0%. (3) Time limit — you must clear wagering within the expiry window (24–72 hours). (4) Max bet — betting above the limit ($5–$10) voids the bonus. (5) Progress tracking — check your dashboard for wagering progress. See our free chips guide for a step-by-step wagering walkthrough.

Game Contribution Rates: Which Games Count?

Game contribution rates determine what percentage of each bet counts toward wagering. Slots/pokies: usually 100% — every $1 bet counts as $1 toward wagering. Table games (blackjack, roulette): 10%–20% — every $1 bet counts as $0.10–$0.20. Live dealer: often 0% — bets don't count at all. Progressive jackpots: often excluded (0%). Specific pokies: some high-RTP or Megaways titles may have reduced rates (50%) or be excluded entirely.

Strategy: always check the eligible games list and contribution rates before claiming. Stick to 100% contribution games to clear wagering fastest. If you want to play table games, know that you'll need to bet 5–10x more to clear the same wagering. Some operators have a "weighted" system where different game categories have different rates. Always verify the current list — it can change. See our high RTP pokies guide for games that typically count 100%.

Expiry Windows: 24–72 Hours & How to Beat Them

Expiry windows specify how long you have to use the bonus and clear wagering. Typical ranges: 24–72 hours for no deposit bonuses, 7–30 days for deposit matches. The clock starts when you claim/activate the bonus, not when you start playing. If you don't use the bonus or clear wagering within the window, the bonus and any winnings are forfeited. Tips to beat expiry: (1) Claim the bonus only when you have time to play — don't claim a 24-hour bonus before bed. (2) Set a phone reminder for the expiry time.
