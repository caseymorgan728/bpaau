Editorial and corrections

If you have spotted a factual error in a review, a broken bonus code, an out-of-date wagering requirement, or an operator page that no longer reflects the current offer, please email us. We aim to fix confirmed factual errors within 48 hours.

Email: editorial@bpaau.org

When emailing, please include the URL of the page in question and a short description of what appears to be wrong. Screenshots or bonus screenshots help.

Operator complaints

BPAAU is not a licensed regulator and cannot force an operator to release winnings or resolve individual account disputes. However, we do take player feedback seriously and use it to update, flag, or remove listings.

Email: support@bpaau.org

Please include: the operator, your registration email (redacted if you prefer), the date, and a clear description of the issue. For unresolved disputes, we recommend also contacting the Gaming Curacao regulator directly and, where relevant, your bank or PayID provider.

Partnerships and media

For affiliate and partnership enquiries, media requests, guest content pitches, or press interviews, please contact:

Email: partners@bpaau.org

Note: BPAAU does not accept paid links, sponsored placements dressed as editorial, or content designed to manipulate rankings.

Response times

  • Editorial corrections: within 48 hours (business days).
  • Operator complaints: within 2 business days.
  • Partnership enquiries: within 5 business days.

Postal enquiries

BPAAU is a digital-only publication and does not maintain a public postal address. All formal correspondence should be sent by email to the addresses above.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I get a response?

Editorial corrections are typically resolved within 48 hours on business days. Operator complaints receive a response within 2 business days. Partnership and media enquiries may take up to 5 business days, especially during peak periods.

Can I report an operator that is not listed on BPAAU?

Yes. If you have had a negative experience with an online casino operator — whether or not we currently list them — email us at support@bpaau.org with the details. We review all submissions and may add the operator to our watchlist or publish a warning if the complaint pattern is serious.

Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content?

BPAAU does not accept paid guest posts, sponsored placements disguised as editorial, or link-building content. We do consider expert contributions from qualified writers in the iGaming space — pitch to partners@bpaau.org with your credentials and a proposed topic.

Can you help me recover funds from an operator?

BPAAU is an independent comparison site, not a regulator or dispute resolution service. We cannot force an operator to release funds. However, we can escalate verified complaints to the operator's affiliate manager and, where appropriate, flag or remove the listing. For formal disputes, contact the Gaming Curacao regulator directly and your payment provider.

How do I suggest a new operator for review?

Email editorial@bpaau.org with the operator name, website, and your reason for suggesting them. We prioritise operators that hold a valid Gaming Curacao licence and support Australian players with PayID.

Need help with a gambling problem? BPAAU cannot provide counselling or clinical support. Free confidential help is available 24/7 at gamblinghelponline.org.au or by calling 1800 858 858.
