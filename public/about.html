Our review methodology

Every operator listed on BPAAU goes through the same six-step review process. We do not accept payment for listings, and commission rates never influence ranking order.

  1. Licence verification. We confirm the operator holds an active Gaming Curacao licence by checking the regulator's public register. We record the licence number, issue date, and status. Operators without a verifiable licence are never listed.
  2. Registration & KYC test. A team member completes full registration, including ID and address verification. We measure KYC approval time and flag any operator that requests excessive documentation or delays verification beyond 48 hours.
  3. Payment channel test. We make a real deposit and withdrawal using each advertised payment method (PayID, bank transfer, crypto). We record processing times, fees, and minimum/maximum limits. PayID withdrawals are tested over three separate cycles.
  4. Bonus term audit. We cross-check every advertised bonus against the operator's published terms. We record wagering multipliers, max cashout caps, expiry windows, game contribution weightings, and max bet rules. Any term that materially disadvantages the player is highlighted in the review.
  5. Game library assessment. Marcus audits the game library for provider coverage, pokie count, RTP distribution, and availability of popular mechanics (Megaways, Hold & Win, progressive jackpots). We test at least 10 games per operator for loading speed and mobile performance.
  6. Support responsiveness test. We contact customer support via live chat and email with a standard set of questions. We measure response time, accuracy, and helpfulness. Operators that fail to respond within 24 hours are flagged.

Our editorial standards

  • Independence. We do not accept payment to alter rankings, remove reviews, or suppress negative findings. Affiliate commissions never influence editorial decisions.
  • Evidence-based. Every claim in a review is backed by a test we performed or a source we cite. We do not reproduce operator marketing copy as fact.
  • Transparency. We disclose affiliate relationships, explain our methodology, and show our scoring criteria. We clearly mark when information is operator-provided vs. independently tested.
  • Corrections. Confirmed factual errors are corrected within 48 hours. Material corrections are noted at the top of the affected page with the date and nature of the change.
  • Responsible gambling. Every page carries an 18+ notice and links to free, confidential gambling support. We do not use language that normalises problem gambling or promises guaranteed wins.
