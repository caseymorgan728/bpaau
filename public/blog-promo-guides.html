Common promotions include welcome bonuses, no-deposit free chips, free spins, reload bonuses, cashback/rebates, VIP programs, daily rewards, and tournament prizes. Each has different terms, wagering requirements, and expiry windows.

Read the full terms and conditions. Look for transparent wagering requirements (under 40x is reasonable), clear max cashout caps, reasonable expiry windows (at least 7 days for deposit bonuses), and no hidden game restrictions. Avoid promotions with 60x+ wagering or 24-hour expiry on deposit bonuses.

A reload bonus is a percentage match on subsequent deposits after the welcome bonus. For example, a 50% reload bonus up to $200 means a $100 deposit gives you $50 in bonus funds. Reload bonuses typically have lower match percentages but similar wagering requirements to welcome offers.
