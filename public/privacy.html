Privacy Policy

Effective 29 August 2026 . Applies to https://bpaau.org and all subdomains

Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit bpaau.org, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

BPAAU is an independent Australian pokies comparison and review website. For the purpose of this policy, BPAAU is the data controller for information collected through the site. Contact: privacy@bpaau.org.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you provide

We only collect information you actively give us - for example, when you email one of our contact addresses. This may include your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit bpaau.org, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our analytics logs may record:

  • Your IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Your browser type, operating system, and device type
  • The pages you view and the referring page
  • The date and time of your visit

3. Cookies

BPAAU uses a small number of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies - used to remember dismissed popups within a session (sessionStorage).
  • Analytics cookies - used to measure aggregate site usage. IP addresses are anonymised.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling them will not prevent you from using bpaau.org.

4. Third-party links and affiliate tracking

bpaau.org contains outbound links to third-party casino operators. When you click one of these links, the destination operator may set its own cookies to track that you arrived from BPAAU. This is standard affiliate tracking and is subject to the destination operator's own privacy policy - which you should read before registering.

5. How we use your data

  • To respond to your emails and support requests.
  • To measure and improve the site's performance and content.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

6. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete that information.
  • Withdraw consent to processing (where processing is based on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@bpaau.org. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Email correspondence is retained for up to 24 months. Server logs are retained for up to 90 days. Anonymised analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

8. Children

bpaau.org is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the version published on this page, dated at the top.
