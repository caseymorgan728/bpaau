About BPAAU for media

Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU) is an independent online pokies comparison and review site serving Australian players. Founded in 2021, BPAAU publishes independently tested operator reviews, original research on the Australian iGaming market, and consumer-focused guides on bonuses, payments, and responsible gambling.

BPAAU is not a gambling operator. We do not hold a gambling licence, accept deposits, or host games. We are an independent publisher funded by affiliate commissions, which never influence our editorial rankings. Our full methodology is published at bpaau.org/methodology, and our annual industry report is available at bpaau.org/research.

Press releases

15 August 2026

BPAAU Publishes 2026 Australian Online Pokies Industry Report

Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU) today released its inaugural Australian Online Pokies Industry Report, combining independently collected operator testing data with public government statistics. The report covers market size, player demographics, PayID adoption trends, bonus term analysis, RTP benchmarks, and responsible gambling data. Key findings include a median PayID withdrawal time of 2–4 hours for verified accounts, a median pokie RTP of 96.2%, and a median no-deposit wagering requirement of 30x. The full report is available at bpaau.org/research.

1 March 2026

BPAAU Expands Editorial Team, Adds Payments and Responsible Gambling Specialists

Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU) announced the expansion of its editorial team with the addition of Sarah Chen (Payments & Compliance Analyst) and Emma Rodriguez (Responsible Gambling Advocate), joining existing team members Daniel Hart (Senior Editor) and Marcus Webb (Game Mechanics Specialist). The expansion reflects BPAAU's commitment to independently tested operator reviews and responsible gambling coverage. All team members' bios are available at bpaau.org/about.

15 January 2026

BPAAU Publishes Transparent Review Methodology

Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU) published its full operator review methodology, detailing the six-step testing process (licence verification, KYC testing, payment channel testing, bonus term audit, game library assessment, and support responsiveness testing) and five-category scoring system. The publication is part of BPAAU's commitment to editorial independence and transparency. The methodology is available at bpaau.org/methodology.

Editorial standards for media reference

When referencing BPAAU in media coverage, please note the following:

  • Attribution: Reference as "Best Pokies Australia (BPAAU)" on first mention, "BPAAU" on subsequent mentions.
  • Data citation: When citing BPAAU research data, attribute to "BPAAU 2026 Australian Online Pokies Industry Report" and link to bpaau.org/research.
  • Independence: BPAAU is an independent comparison site, not a gambling operator. We do not hold a gambling licence.
  • Funding: BPAAU is funded by affiliate commissions, which never influence editorial rankings. Our methodology is publicly published.
  • 18+ notice: All BPAAU content is intended for adults aged 18+. Please include an 18+ notice when republishing BPAAU content.
