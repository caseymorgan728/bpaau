Our commitment

Gambling is entertainment for most people, and a serious problem for some. BPAAU only lists operators that are licensed and audited, but a licence does not remove the risk of harm. This page exists so that any visitor to the Site can quickly find real help.

Warning signs

You or someone you know may have a gambling problem if you notice:

  • Spending more time or money on gambling than you planned.
  • Chasing losses - betting more to try to win back what you have lost.
  • Borrowing money, using credit cards, or selling belongings to gamble.
  • Lying to family or friends about how much you gamble.
  • Feeling anxious, depressed, or irritable when trying to cut down.
  • Neglecting work, study, relationships, or health because of gambling.

If any of these apply, please reach out to one of the support services below. They are free, confidential, and available around the clock.

Free 24/7 support in Australia

  • Gambling Help Online - 1800 858 858 . gamblinghelponline.org.au - free counselling, live chat, and email support.
  • Lifeline - 13 11 14 . lifeline.org.au - crisis support if you are in emotional distress.
  • Beyond Blue - 1300 22 4636 . beyondblue.org.au - for gambling-related depression and anxiety.
  • Gamblers Anonymous Australia - gaaustralia.org.au - peer-support meetings across Australia.
  • Financial Counselling Australia - 1800 007 007 (National Debt Helpline) - free help with debt caused by gambling.

Self-exclusion

Australia offers a national self-exclusion register for online gambling called BetStop. If you register, all Australian-licensed online gambling operators are required to close your accounts and stop marketing to you for the period you choose (from 3 months to lifetime).

Register free at betstop.gov.au.

Individual operators listed on BPAAU also offer their own self-exclusion tools inside their account settings. Look for "Responsible Gaming", "Self-Exclusion", or "Account Limits".

Practical tools that help

  • Deposit limits. Set a daily, weekly, or monthly deposit cap before you play - most operators let you set this in Account Settings.
  • Time limits. Some operators offer session-length limits; use them.
  • Reality checks. Enable pop-ups that remind you how long you have been playing.
  • Cool-off periods. Most operators offer 24-hour to 6-week cool-off breaks separate from full self-exclusion.
  • Bank-level blocks. Some Australian banks (including Commonwealth Bank, NAB, and Westpac) let you block gambling transactions on your card via the banking app.

Under 18?

Online gambling is illegal in Australia for anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please leave this site. Parents concerned about a child accessing gambling content can install filtering software such as GamBlock or BetFilter.

Support for family and friends

Problem gambling affects families as much as the person gambling. Gambling Help Online offers dedicated support for partners, parents, and children of people with a gambling problem - call 1800 858 858 or visit gamblinghelponline.org.au/i-am-worried-about-someone.
